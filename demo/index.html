About The Platform

How NEXUS AI Works

01 — Telemetry & Detection

Operational metrics are processed through an Isolation Forest anomaly detector and critical threshold rules. Observation confirmation and cooldown logic reduce unnecessary duplicate incidents.

02 — Incident Intelligence

Detected anomalies become incident records containing service information, severity, supporting evidence, and an event timeline.

03 — AI Investigation

An investigation engine analyzes incident evidence, proposes probable causes, and generates actionable recommendations with an assessment confidence level.

04 — Controlled Remediation

Administrators review findings and approve or reject remediation. The current system supports safe simulation, rather than real infrastructure changes.
